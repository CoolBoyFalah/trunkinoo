How to Use

Your perfect coffee in the easiest way. No machine. No barista. Just you.

01
Open the Bag
Open along the perforation carefully to preserve the drip bag inside.
02
Expand the Drip
Unfold and expand the drip bag wings so it sits securely on your cup.
03
Pour Hot Water
Slowly pour 150ml of hot water over the drip bag. Let it bloom and drip.
04
Enjoy Your Coffee
Remove the bag, sit back, and enjoy your perfectly brewed Trunkino cup.
